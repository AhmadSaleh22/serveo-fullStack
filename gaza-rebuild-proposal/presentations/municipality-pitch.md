## The Challenge We Face

### Gaza After the War

- **2+ million** people affected
- **80%+** of buildings damaged or destroyed
- **Hospitals** overwhelmed or non-functional
- **Schools** destroyed
- **Records** lost - birth certificates, property deeds, medical records
- **Economy** collapsed - 80%+ unemployment
- **Families** separated, searching for loved ones

### The Coordination Problem

- Aid organizations working in silos
- No central data on who needs what
- Reconstruction happening without coordination
- No way to track progress
- Citizens can't access services

---

## The Opportunity

### Technology Can Accelerate Recovery

What takes years with paper can take months with digital systems.

| Challenge | Paper/Traditional | Digital Solution |
|-----------|-------------------|------------------|
| Aid distribution | Chaotic, duplicates, fraud | Fair, tracked, verified |
| Finding missing persons | Word of mouth, weeks | Database, hours |
| Medical coordination | Phone calls, guesswork | Real-time dashboard |
| Property claims | Years of disputes | Verified in weeks |
| Reconstruction tracking | Unknown progress | Live dashboard |

## Why Digital First?

### 1. Speed
- Paper registration: 10 families/hour
- Digital registration: 100 families/hour

### 2. Accuracy
- Paper: Errors, lost records, duplicates
- Digital: Verified, searchable, no duplicates

### 3. Transparency
- Paper: Who received what? Unknown
- Digital: Full audit trail, donor reports

### 4. Resilience
- Paper: Burns, floods, gets lost
- Digital: Backed up, distributed, survives

### 5. Scalability
- Paper: Need more people for more work
- Digital: Same team serves millions

## Design for Gaza's Reality

### We understand the constraints:

**No reliable internet?**
→ Offline-first design. Everything works without connection.

**No reliable power?**
→ Low-power apps. Solar-powered servers. Battery optimization.

**No smartphones for everyone?**
→ SMS and USSD support. Feature phone compatible.

**Limited technical skills?**
→ Simple interfaces. Arabic-first. Training included.

**Security concerns?**
→ Encrypted data. Privacy controls. Audit trails.
