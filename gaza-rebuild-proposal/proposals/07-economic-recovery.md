# Economic Recovery Platform
## Priority: P3 (Long-term Stability)

---

## The Problem

Gaza's economy is devastated:
- 80%+ unemployment
- Businesses destroyed
- Banks damaged, cash scarce
- No way to send/receive money
- Skilled workers can't find work
- Farmers can't sell crops
- Markets destroyed
- No business registration system

**A carpenter's workshop was destroyed. He has his skills but no tools, no customers, no way to restart.**

---

## The Solution: "Iqtisad" (اقتصاد) - Economic Recovery Platform

A digital ecosystem to restart Gaza's economy from the ground up.

### Core Components:

#### 1. Digital Wallet & Payments
- Mobile money for everyone
- Send/receive without banks
- Pay for goods and services
- Receive aid and wages

#### 2. Job Matching
- Connect workers with opportunities
- Skill verification
- Reconstruction job board
- Freelance marketplace

#### 3. Business Registry
- Register businesses digitally
- Micro-loan applications
- Supplier connections
- Customer discovery

#### 4. Marketplace
- Buy/sell platform
- Local products
- Services directory
- Delivery coordination

#### 5. Skills & Training
- Online courses
- Certification programs
- Apprenticeship matching
- Career guidance

---

## Digital Wallet System

### The Challenge:
- Banks destroyed or inaccessible
- Cash is scarce and dangerous to carry
- No way to receive international transfers
- Aid distribution is cash-based and chaotic

### The Solution: "Gaza Pay"

```

```

### How It Works:
```
1. Registration:
   - Sign up with phone number
   - Verify identity (linked to Digital ID)
   - Get instant wallet

2. Add Money:
   - Receive from aid organizations
   - Receive from family abroad
   - Get paid for work
   - Cash-in at agents

3. Spend Money:
   - Pay at shops (QR code)
   - Send to family/friends
   - Pay for services
   - Cash-out at agents

4. Agent Network:
   - Local shops become agents
   - Handle cash-in/cash-out
   - Earn commission
   - 500+ agents across Gaza
```
